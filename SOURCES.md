# Data & Scientific Sources — Moquegua Wildfire Case Study

1. **NASA Earthdata / Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF Vertex):** Sentinel-1A/B C-Band Interferometric Wide Swath (IW) Ground Range Detected High-Resolution (`GRDH`) dual-polarization (`VV+VH`) scenes over Mariscal Nieto, Moquegua (`-71.5, -17.8, -70.0, -16.0`).
2. **NASA FIRMS (Fire Information for Resource Management System):** VIIRS S-NPP / NOAA-20 375m active fire thermal anomalies.
3. **COER Moquegua & INDECI (SINPAD):** Regional Emergency Operations Center situational reports for November 2026 wildfire incidents in San Antonio (Quebrada El Rayo, Huaracanito), Omo Valley, Samegua, and Torata.
4. **SERFOR Peru:** National Forest and Wildlife Service satellite hotspot monitoring bulletins for southern Peru.
